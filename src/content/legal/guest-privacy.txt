PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the vacation-rental website for Tiki Cottage Lake Texoma (the “Property”), operated by Texoma Tiki (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, shared, retained, and protected when you visit this website, submit an inquiry, request a booking, make a payment, or otherwise interact with us through the website.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you submit an inquiry or booking request, we may collect information including:

• Your name
• Email address
• Telephone number
• Check-in and check-out dates
• Number of adults, children, infants, or pets, where applicable
• Special requests or messages
• Booking and reservation information
• Payment and refund status
• Other information you voluntarily provide

Guests are not required to create an account to browse the website, submit an inquiry, or request a booking.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We may use information collected through the website to:

• Respond to inquiries
• Review and process booking requests
• Communicate with you regarding your reservation
• Manage check-in and check-out
• Process or facilitate payments and refunds
• Handle cancellations or reservation changes
• Provide information about the Property
• Maintain booking and business records
• Prevent fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activity
• Resolve disputes
• Comply with applicable legal, accounting, tax, or regulatory requirements
• Enforce applicable booking terms and property policies

3. PAYMENTS

When online payment processing is available, payment-card information is entered and processed through an independent third-party payment processor.

Texoma Tiki and this website do not store your complete credit-card number.

The reservation system may retain payment-related information necessary to administer your reservation, including payment status, amount paid, refunded amount, refund status, and transaction identifiers.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We may use third-party service providers to support the operation of this website and reservation system.

These providers may support functions such as:

• Website hosting
• Database and secure data storage
• Payment processing and refunds
• Transactional email delivery
• Booking administration
• Calendar synchronization
• Security and technical infrastructure
• Other services necessary to operate the website and reservation system

These providers may process information as reasonably necessary to provide services on our behalf.

Their handling of information may also be governed by their own privacy policies, terms, and security practices.

5. SHARING OF INFORMATION

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, administer bookings, process payments or refunds, deliver transactional communications, maintain security, or provide requested services.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

• Comply with applicable law or legal process
• Respond to lawful governmental requests
• Prevent or investigate fraud or misuse
• Protect the rights, safety, security, or property of guests, Texoma Tiki, or others
• Resolve disputes
• Enforce applicable agreements or property policies

6. TECHNOLOGY PROVIDERS

The website and reservation-management technology may be provided or supported by independent technology providers acting on behalf of Texoma Tiki.

Such providers may process guest information as reasonably necessary to provide hosting, booking management, communications, payment integrations, security, maintenance, and related technology services.

Texoma Tiki remains responsible for the guest relationship and for determining how guest information is used in connection with the rental Property.

7. COOKIES, ANALYTICS, AND TRACKING

The current website does not use advertising cookies, behavioral advertising trackers, third-party tracking pixels, or similar technologies for behavioral advertising.

Technical systems necessary to operate and secure the website may be used.

If additional analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

8. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and for legitimate booking, business, accounting, payment, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, and legal purposes.

Certain booking or transaction records may be retained for up to seven years where reasonably necessary for accounting, tax, payment, dispute, fraud-prevention, or legal purposes.

Information that is no longer reasonably necessary may be deleted, anonymized, or otherwise removed.

9. SECURITY

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to help protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no website, database, payment system, network, or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights relating to your personal information, which may include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

Privacy-related requests may be submitted to:

texomatiki@gmail.com

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, maintain transaction records, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or protect the security and integrity of the reservation system.

11. CHILDREN AND MINIMUM BOOKING AGE

The person submitting a booking request must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into a rental agreement.

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children for the purpose of entering into a rental agreement.

Information about children or infants included as members of a reservation party may be provided by the adult making the reservation where necessary to administer the booking.

12. EMAIL COMMUNICATIONS

By submitting an inquiry or booking request, you acknowledge that transactional emails may be sent to the email address you provide concerning your inquiry, reservation, payment, cancellation, refund, check-in, check-out, or stay.

These communications relate to your inquiry or reservation and do not by themselves constitute consent to unrelated promotional marketing.

13. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the Effective Date shown at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.
