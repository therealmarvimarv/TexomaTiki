WEBDEVSTERS VACATION RENTAL MANAGEMENT PLATFORM
OWNER & ADMIN DOCUMENTATION

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

This guide explains how to use the Webdevsters Vacation Rental Management Platform to manage your vacation-rental website, bookings, availability, pricing, payments, guest communications, and property information.

The Platform is designed so that most day-to-day property management can be handled directly from the Admin Dashboard without editing website code.

==================================================
1. ACCESSING THE ADMIN DASHBOARD
==================================================

Administrative access is limited to authorized users.

Use the login information provided to you to access your Admin Dashboard.

Your account must be authorized before access is granted. Simply having login credentials does not automatically provide access unless the account has also been approved for the property.

Keep your login credentials private.

If you believe your account has been compromised, contact your administrator or Webdevsters as soon as possible.

==================================================
2. ADMIN DASHBOARD OVERVIEW
==================================================

The Admin Dashboard contains the main tools used to manage your property.

Depending on your configuration, the main navigation includes:

• Overview
• Calendar
• Bookings
• Cleaning
• Maintenance
• Property
• Email
• Payments
• Account

The Account area also provides access to:

• Privacy Policy
• Terms & Conditions
• Documentation

==================================================
3. OVERVIEW
==================================================

The Overview page provides a quick summary of activity related to your property.

Use this area to get a general view of your rental before moving into the more detailed management sections.

==================================================
4. CALENDAR & AVAILABILITY
==================================================

The Calendar is used to manage when the property is available or unavailable.

You may use the calendar to review:

• Confirmed bookings
• Pending bookings
• Owner blocks
• Manually blocked dates
• Availability
• Pricing information

Dates may also be affected by bookings or calendar information synchronized from external booking platforms.

OWNER BLOCKS

Use an Owner Block when you want to reserve dates for yourself or otherwise prevent guests from booking them.

BLOCKED DATES

Dates can also be blocked for maintenance, private use, or other reasons.

Always review the calendar after making availability changes.

==================================================
5. CALENDAR SYNCHRONIZATION
==================================================

The Platform supports iCal calendar synchronization with compatible external booking platforms.

This allows availability to be shared between your direct-booking website and other booking services.

Important:

External booking platforms control how frequently they check imported calendar feeds.

Changes made in this Platform may therefore appear here immediately but may take additional time to appear on another booking platform.

Calendar synchronization should not be treated as instantaneous.

Regularly review your calendars when managing reservations across multiple platforms.

==================================================
6. BOOKINGS
==================================================

The Bookings section is used to review and manage guest booking requests and reservations.

A typical booking may move through statuses such as:

• Pending
• Approved
• Pending Payment
• Confirmed
• Declined
• Cancelled
• Expired
• Payment Failed
• Refunded
• Partially Refunded

BOOKING REQUESTS

When a guest submits a booking request, review the reservation information before taking action.

Information may include:

• Guest name
• Email
• Phone number
• Check-in date
• Check-out date
• Adults
• Children
• Infants
• Pets
• Guest message or special request
• Price and fees

APPROVING A BOOKING

If the request is acceptable, approve it using the available booking controls.

If online payment is required, the booking may then move into a payment stage before becoming confirmed.

DECLINING A BOOKING

If you do not wish to accept the booking, use the decline option.

CANCELLATIONS

Cancellation and refund decisions are separate actions.

Cancelling a reservation does not automatically mean that money has been refunded unless the applicable refund process has also been completed.

==================================================
7. PAYMENTS
==================================================

The Payments section controls how direct-booking payments are handled.

Depending on your configuration, payments may be processed electronically or handled manually.

If online card payments are enabled, guests are directed to a secure third-party payment checkout.

The Platform does not store guests' complete credit-card numbers.

Payment information shown inside the Platform may include:

• Payment status
• Amount paid
• Payment session information
• Refund status
• Refunded amount

PAYMENT SETTINGS

Only modify payment settings when necessary.

Incorrect payment credentials or settings can prevent guests from completing payment.

TESTING

If a test payment mode is available, use it when testing payment functionality rather than processing real charges.

LIVE PAYMENTS

Before accepting real bookings, make sure the correct live payment configuration is active.

==================================================
8. REFUNDS
==================================================

Refunds should be handled according to your property's published cancellation and refund policy.

Depending on your payment configuration, refunds may be processed through the Platform's supported payment workflow or through the connected payment provider.

The Platform may track:

• Full refunds
• Partial refunds
• Total amount refunded
• Updated payment status

Always confirm that the refund has been successfully processed before telling a guest that the refund is complete.

==================================================
9. PRICING
==================================================

Pricing is managed from the Property pricing tools.

Depending on the configuration, pricing may include:

• Default nightly rate
• Day-of-week rates
• Date-specific price overrides
• Seasonal pricing
• Minimum-night requirements
• Cleaning fees
• Pet fees
• Extra-guest fees
• Other property fees

DEFAULT NIGHTLY RATE

The default nightly rate is used when no more specific pricing rule applies.

DAY-OF-WEEK PRICING

You may assign different rates to specific days of the week.

If no custom rate is entered for a particular day, the default nightly rate is used.

DATE-SPECIFIC PRICING

Use date-specific pricing when particular dates require a special rate.

Always review the public website after major pricing changes.

==================================================

Enter accurate coordinates if you want the map pin to represent the property's correct location.

Do not rely on a city or location name alone to position the map pin.

==================================================
12. PHOTOS
==================================================

Use the property photo tools to manage images shown to guests.

Depending on the section, you may be able to:

• Add images
• Remove images
• Reorder images
• Assign images to photo sections

Use clear, current photographs that accurately represent the property.

==================================================
13. AMENITIES
==================================================

Amenities describe features available at the property.

Review the amenity list periodically and keep it accurate.

Do not advertise an amenity that is unavailable, removed, or temporarily out of service without clearly informing guests.

==================================================
14. POLICIES
==================================================

The Policies area contains important guest-facing information.

Built-in policy areas may include:

• Cancellation Policy
• Check-In & Check-Out
• Pet Policy
• Accessibility Notes

CANCELLATION POLICY

Keep your cancellation and refund terms current and consistent with the Terms & Conditions provided to guests.

CHECK-IN & CHECK-OUT

You may manage information such as:

• Check-in time
• Check-out time
• Early check-in notes
• Late check-out notes
• Parking information
• Property access information

PET POLICY

You may manage information such as:

• Whether pets are allowed
• Maximum number of pets
• Pet fees
• Furniture or property restrictions

ACCESSIBILITY NOTES

Only enter accessibility information you know to be accurate.

Information may include:

• Property level
• Number of entry steps
• Bedroom floor
• Parking distance
• Accessibility notes
• Certification information

Do not make assumptions about accessibility features.

If information is unknown, leave it unspecified.

18. CLEANING
==================================================

The Cleaning section can be used to manage cleaning-related tasks connected to property operations.

Use this section to review and maintain cleaning activity associated with upcoming or completed stays.

Keep operational notes clear so anyone assisting with the property understands what needs to be completed.

==================================================
19. MAINTENANCE
==================================================

The Maintenance section is used to track property-maintenance information and internal notes.

Use it to document issues that require attention.

Guests should not rely on the Maintenance section because it is intended for administrative use.

==================================================
20. EMAIL & NOTIFICATIONS
==================================================

The Platform can send transactional emails related to bookings and property activity.

Depending on your configuration, emails may include:

• Booking request notifications
• Booking approval or decline messages
• Payment-related messages
• Confirmation emails
• Cancellation messages
• Refund notifications
• Other automated guest or host communications

EMAIL SETTINGS

The Email section controls your email configuration and available email templates.

Be careful when changing email-provider credentials or technical email settings.

EMAIL TEMPLATES

Templates may contain variables that automatically insert booking or guest information.

Do not remove required template variables unless you understand how they are being used.

==================================================
22. ADMIN USERS & ACCESS
==================================================

Administrative access is restricted.

Authorized users may include the property owner, approved administrators, or approved developers.

Admin and developer users may have similar access to property-management functionality depending on the Platform configuration.

Do not create or share access with someone who should not be able to manage the property.

If another person needs regular administrative access, request a separate authorized account rather than sharing credentials whenever possible.

==================================================
23. PRIVACY POLICY & TERMS & CONDITIONS
==================================================

The Account area provides access to the Platform Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

These documents apply to use of the administrative Platform.

The public property website has separate guest-facing Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions documents.

The guest documents and administrative documents are separate and should not be confused with one another.

==================================================
24. PUBLIC WEBSITE LEGAL DOCUMENTS
==================================================

The public property website contains separate:

• Privacy Policy
• Terms & Conditions

These documents apply to guests and visitors using the public website.

Property-specific information such as cancellation terms, booking age, contact information, and other guest policies should remain accurate.

Contact Webdevsters if your legal or business information changes and the website documents need to be updated.

==================================================
25. SECURITY BEST PRACTICES
==================================================

To help protect your property and guest information:

• Use a strong password.
• Do not share your password unnecessarily.
• Log out when using a shared or public computer.
• Notify Webdevsters if you believe an account has been compromised.
• Do not send payment credentials or sensitive passwords through insecure messages.
• Review administrative access when staff or business relationships change.

==================================================
26. BEFORE MAKING MAJOR CHANGES
==================================================

Before making significant changes to:

• Payments
• Email configuration
• Pricing
• Availability
• Booking policies
• Website settings

make sure you understand what the setting controls.

If you are unsure about a technical setting, contact Webdevsters before changing it.

==================================================
27. REVIEW CHANGES AFTER SAVING
==================================================

After updating guest-facing information, review the public website.

Confirm that:

• Text is correct
• Pricing is correct
• Dates are correct
• Photos display correctly
• Policies are accurate
• Contact information is current
• Links work
• Mobile display looks correct

A successful Save confirms that the information was stored, but you should still review the public result when making important changes.

==================================================
28. SUPPORT
==================================================

If you experience a problem with the Platform:

1. Note what you were attempting to do.
2. Record any error message you received.
3. Take a screenshot when helpful.
4. Avoid repeatedly changing unrelated settings.
5. Contact Webdevsters or use the support information provided in your Account section.

Providing clear details about the issue helps troubleshooting happen faster.

==================================================
29. PLATFORM UPDATES
==================================================

Webdevsters may periodically update the Platform to improve functionality, security, performance, or usability.

Certain features or interface elements may change over time.

This Documentation may also be updated as new features are added or existing features change.

==================================================
30. FINAL REMINDERS
==================================================

The Admin Dashboard is designed to give you control over the day-to-day operation of your vacation-rental website.

For routine management:

• Keep your calendar current.
• Review booking requests promptly.
• Keep pricing accurate.
• Keep property information current.
• Review payments before confirming financial matters with guests.
• Maintain accurate policies and house rules.
• Periodically review your public website.
• Protect your administrative login credentials.

When in doubt about a technical or security-related setting, contact Webdevsters before making the change.
